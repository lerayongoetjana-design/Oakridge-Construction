About Oakridge

Twenty-five years of building with purpose

A proudly South African contractor, built on the belief that great buildings come from great relationships.

Our Story

From a team of twelve to a national builder

Oakridge began in 1998 with a simple conviction: that clients deserve a contractor who is as invested in the result as they are.

What started as a small residential builder in Johannesburg has grown into one of South Africa’s most respected construction companies, with more than 1,200 professionals across offices in Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban.

Through every stage of that growth, our principles have remained unchanged. We invest in our people, uphold the highest standards of safety, and measure success not by what we complete, but by what endures.

Mission & Vision

Shaping the spaces where South Africa lives and works

Our mission is to deliver exceptional buildings safely, sustainably and with complete integrity — creating lasting value for our clients, our people and our communities.

Our vision is to be Africa’s most trusted builder of landmark spaces, recognised as much for how we build as for what we build.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped us

1998

Founded in Johannesburg

David Whitfield establishes Oakridge as a specialist residential builder with a team of twelve.

2004

CIDB Grade 9 Status

Oakridge achieves the highest contractor grading, opening the door to major commercial work.

2009

Cape Town Office Opens

Expansion into the Western Cape, delivering luxury residential and hospitality projects.

2015

First Green Star Building

Completion of our first 5-Star Green Star rated office development in Rosebank.

2019

Durban Office & Level 1 B-BBEE

A third regional office and Level 1 B-BBEE status, cementing our national footprint.

2024

R12 Billion Delivered

Passing 340 completed projects and R12 billion in total value constructed.
