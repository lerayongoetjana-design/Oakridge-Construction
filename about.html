About Oakridge

Building with purpose since 1998

The story of a fictional South African contractor, built on the belief that great buildings come from great relationships.

Our Story

From a small team to a national builder

Oakridge began with a simple conviction: that clients deserve a builder who is as invested in the result as they are.

What started as a residential builder in Johannesburg grew into a construction company with offices in Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban, working across homes, workplaces and industrial facilities.

Through that growth, the principles stayed the same: invest in people, put safety first, and measure success not by what we finish, but by what endures.

Our Values

The principles behind every project

Our values are not words on a wall. They guide how we price, plan, build and behave, on every site and in every meeting.

Integrity

We say what we will do and do what we say. Honest pricing, open reporting and no surprises.

Precision

Every tolerance matters. We plan meticulously and build carefully, from foundations to finishes.

Safety First

Everyone goes home safe, every day. Safety is the first agenda item on every site, every morning.

Lasting Legacy

We build for the next generation: durable, efficient spaces that add value to their communities.

Mission & Vision

Shaping the spaces where South Africa lives and works

Our mission is to deliver exceptional buildings safely, sustainably and with complete integrity, creating lasting value for our clients, our people and our communities.

Our vision is to be known as much for how we build as for what we build.

  • Apprenticeships and artisan training on our sites
  • Partnering with local enterprises in the communities where we build
  • Responsible material sourcing and waste reduction on every project
Our Journey

Milestones that shaped us

1998

Founded in Johannesburg

Oakridge begins as a small residential builder in Johannesburg’s northern suburbs.

2005

Into Commercial Work

The company takes on its first office and retail buildings alongside its residential projects.

2009

Cape Town Office

A Western Cape office opens to serve residential and hospitality clients in the region.

2014

Industrial Division

A dedicated team forms to build warehouses, logistics and manufacturing facilities.

2019

Durban Office

A KwaZulu-Natal office in Umhlanga completes a three-region footprint.

Today

Six Integrated Services

Residential, commercial, industrial, renovation, property development and project management under one roof.

How We Work

Standards we build to

National Building Regulations

Every design and build is planned around SANS 10400 and local municipal approval requirements.

Health & Safety

Site practice follows the Occupational Health and Safety Act and the Construction Regulations.

Clear Contracts

Projects are structured on recognised industry contract forms, with scope and variations agreed in writing.

Responsible Building

Energy-efficient design, careful material sourcing and on-site waste reduction are built into planning.
