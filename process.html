Our Process

A clear path from vision to handover

Six disciplined stages that give you certainty on cost, time and quality.

At a Glance

Certainty at every step

Construction is complex. Our process is designed to make it feel simple, with one accountable team, clear milestones and complete transparency from the first meeting to the final key.

Consult

We begin by listening: understanding your vision, site, budget and timeline, and testing feasibility before a single line is drawn.

Plan & Design

Architects, engineers and quantity surveyors align early, producing a buildable design, a clear programme and a transparent cost plan.

Build

Our site teams work with discipline: safety first, weekly reporting and careful quality control at every stage.

Handover

We hand over complete, commissioned and documented, then stand behind our work with planned aftercare and maintenance.

Stage by Stage

How we deliver your project

Stage 01  ·  Weeks 1 – 2

Consultation & Brief

Every successful project begins with listening. We meet to understand your vision, functional needs, budget expectations and the reasons behind the project.

A senior director is assigned from day one and stays accountable until handover, so you always know who is responsible.

  • Site visit
  • Client brief
  • Stakeholder mapping
  • Initial budget range
Stage 02  ·  Weeks 2 – 6

Feasibility & Cost Planning

Before design begins in earnest, we test what is possible: zoning, ground conditions, services and the municipal approvals the project will need.

Quantity surveyors prepare a cost plan and realistic programme, so your investment decision rests on evidence rather than assumptions.

  • Site investigation
  • Zoning review
  • Cost plan
  • Risk register
Stage 03  ·  Months 2 – 6

Design & Pre-Construction

Architects, engineers and our construction team work together from the outset, using coordinated digital models to resolve clashes on screen, not on site.

Value engineering protects the design intent while improving buildability, and we manage the approval process on your behalf.

  • Model coordination
  • Value engineering
  • Council approvals
  • Agreed programme
Stage 04  ·  Months 5 – 7

Procurement & Mobilisation

Every package is tendered competitively through vetted suppliers and subcontractors, with a focus on quality, reliability and local enterprise.

Before breaking ground, the team completes the health and safety file, site establishment, logistics plan and neighbour communication.

  • Subcontract tenders
  • Local suppliers
  • Safety file
  • Site establishment
Stage 05  ·  Project dependent

Construction

Site teams work with discipline. Daily safety briefings, weekly progress meetings and monthly cost reports keep everyone aligned.

Quality inspections at every hold point, from foundations to finishes, make sure the building is delivered as designed.

  • Weekly reporting
  • Quality hold points
  • Progress photographs
  • Cost control
Stage 06  ·  Completion onwards

Handover & Aftercare

We hand over a building that is complete, commissioned and documented, with operating manuals, warranties and as-built drawings.

An aftercare team remains available through the defects period and beyond, with planned maintenance available for the life of the building.

  • Commissioning
  • As-built documents
  • Defects period
  • Maintenance plans
Our Commitments

What sets our process apart

Behind every stage sit the standards and systems that keep projects safe, on budget and on time.

Transparent Reporting

Weekly progress reports, monthly cost statements and regular site meetings mean you always know where your project stands.

Safety Without Compromise

A site-specific safety plan, daily toolbox talks and a dedicated safety officer on every site.

Quality Assurance

Inspection and test plans with sign-off at every hold point, from foundations to final finishes.

Programme Certainty

Detailed programming, early ordering of long-lead items and active risk management keep projects on time.

Sustainable Building

Energy-efficient design choices, responsible material sourcing and waste-reduction targets on site.

Local Empowerment

Working with local enterprises and offering artisan training in the communities where we build.

FAQ

Working with us

Common questions about how projects are run.

Contact Us
How early should we involve Oakridge?

As early as possible. Involving a builder during feasibility usually leads to better design decisions, more reliable budgets and a smoother programme.

How will we be kept informed during construction?

Through weekly progress reports with photographs, monthly cost reports and regular site meetings with your project director.

What happens if the scope changes?

Every variation is priced and agreed in writing before work proceeds, with its effect on the programme clearly stated.

What aftercare do you provide?

Each project includes a defects period after handover, during which the aftercare team responds to any issues. Planned maintenance is available afterwards.
