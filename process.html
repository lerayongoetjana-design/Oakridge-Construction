Our Process

A clear path from vision to handover

Six disciplined stages, refined over 340 projects, that give you certainty on cost, time and quality.

At a Glance

Certainty at every step

Construction is complex. Our process is designed to make it feel simple — with one accountable team, clear milestones and complete transparency from the first meeting to the final key.

Consult

We begin by listening — understanding your vision, site, budget and timeline, and testing feasibility before a single line is drawn.

Plan & Design

Architects, engineers and quantity surveyors align early, producing a buildable design, a fixed programme and a transparent cost plan.

Build

Our site teams execute with discipline — rigorous safety, weekly reporting and uncompromising quality control at every stage.

Handover

We deliver complete, commissioned and documented — then stand behind our work with comprehensive aftercare and maintenance.

Stage by Stage

How we deliver your project

Stage 01  ·  Week 1 – 2

Consultation & Brief

Every successful project begins with listening. We meet with you to understand your vision, functional requirements, budget expectations and the commercial drivers behind the development.

A senior director is assigned from day one and remains accountable until handover — you will always know exactly who is responsible for your project.

  • Site visit
  • Client brief
  • Stakeholder mapping
  • Initial budget range
Stage 02  ·  Week 2 – 6

Feasibility & Cost Planning

Before design begins in earnest, we test what is possible. Our team reviews zoning, geotechnical conditions, bulk services and municipal approvals to confirm the project is viable.

Our in-house quantity surveyors produce an elemental cost plan and a realistic programme, so your investment decision is based on facts rather than assumptions.

  • Site investigation
  • Zoning review
  • Elemental cost plan
  • Risk register
Stage 03  ·  Month 2 – 6

Design & Pre-Construction

Architects, engineers and our construction team work together from the outset. We use BIM coordination to resolve clashes on screen, not on site.

Value engineering sessions protect the design intent while improving buildability, and we manage the full municipal approval process on your behalf.

  • BIM coordination
  • Value engineering
  • Council approvals
  • Fixed programme
Stage 04  ·  Month 5 – 7

Procurement & Mobilisation

We tender every package competitively through our vetted supply chain, prioritising quality, reliability and local enterprise development.

Before breaking ground, our site team completes the health and safety file, site establishment, logistics plan and community engagement.

  • Subcontract tenders
  • SMME development
  • Health & safety file
  • Site establishment
Stage 05  ·  Project dependent

Construction

Our site teams execute with discipline. Daily safety briefings, weekly progress meetings and monthly cost reports keep everyone aligned and informed.

Independent quality inspections at every hold point — from foundations to finishes — ensure the building is delivered exactly as designed.

  • Weekly reporting
  • Quality hold points
  • Live client dashboard
  • Cost control
Stage 06  ·  Completion +12 months

Handover & Aftercare

We hand over a building that is complete, commissioned and fully documented, with operation manuals, warranties and as-built drawings.

Our aftercare team remains on call throughout the defects liability period and beyond, with planned maintenance available for the life of the building.

  • Commissioning
  • As-built documentation
  • Defects liability
  • Maintenance plans

FAQ

Working with us

Common questions about how we run projects. For anything else, our team is a call away.

Contact Us
How early should we involve Oakridge?

As early as possible. Involving a contractor at feasibility stage typically saves 5–10% on construction cost and several months on programme through better design decisions.

How will we be kept informed during construction?

You will receive weekly progress reports with photographs, monthly cost reports and access to our live client dashboard, plus regular site meetings with your project director.

What happens if the scope changes?

Every variation is priced and agreed in writing before work proceeds, with its impact on programme clearly stated. There are no surprises on the final account.

What aftercare do you provide?

All projects include a defects liability period, typically 12 months, during which our aftercare team responds to any issues. Planned maintenance contracts are available thereafter.
