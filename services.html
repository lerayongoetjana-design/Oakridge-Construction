Contact Us
How accurate is the cost calculator?

It is illustrative only. It uses indicative rates per square metre to show a broad range in rand. A real quotation depends on design, site conditions, specification and current market prices.

What size of project do you take on?

Oakridge’s fictional services cover everything from home renovations to large commercial and industrial buildings.

Do you offer fixed-price contracts?

Depending on the project, contracts can be structured as lump-sum, guaranteed maximum price or cost-plus, using recognised industry contract forms.

How do you manage health and safety?

Every site has a safety plan, a dedicated safety officer and daily toolbox talks, with work planned around the Occupational Health and Safety Act and Construction Regulations.

Can you help before we have an architect?

Yes. Through property development and project management services, Oakridge can help assemble the professional team, test feasibility and develop the brief.
