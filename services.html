Contact Us
Which regions of South Africa do you work in?

We operate nationally from our offices in Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban, with completed projects in all nine provinces. For larger developments we also mobilise to neighbouring SADC countries.

What size of project do you take on?

Our commercial and industrial work typically ranges from R20 million to over R1 billion. For luxury private residences, projects usually start from R8 million.

Do you offer fixed-price contracts?

Yes. Depending on the project we work under JBCC, NEC and FIDIC contracts, including lump-sum, guaranteed maximum price and cost-plus arrangements.

How do you manage health and safety?

We are ISO 45001 certified and fully compliant with the Construction Regulations of 2014. Every site has a dedicated safety officer and daily toolbox talks.

Can you help before we have an architect?

Absolutely. Through our design-and-build service we can assemble the professional team, test feasibility and develop the brief with you from the very beginning.
