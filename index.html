FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before starting a project with Oakridge.

Ask Our Team
What types of projects does Oakridge undertake?

Oakridge offers residential, commercial and industrial construction, renovations and refurbishments, property development and construction project management.

Which areas of South Africa do you cover?

The fictional Oakridge operates from offices in Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban, serving clients across Gauteng, the Western Cape and KwaZulu-Natal.

How do I request a quote?

Use the quotation form on the contact page or the WhatsApp enquiry button. On this demonstration site, nothing you enter is sent or stored.

Can I get an idea of cost before I enquire?

Yes. The cost calculator on our services page gives an illustrative range in South African rand based on project type, size, specification and region.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scale, design and approvals. As a general guide, a new home often takes 10 to 18 months and a commercial building 12 to 30 months.

What happens after my project is completed?

Each project ends with commissioning, a full handover pack and an agreed aftercare period, with planned maintenance available afterwards.
