About this experience

Built in code, rendered live

The Sandton Exchange and its surroundings are original 3D models created for this demonstration and rendered in real time in your browser with Three.js and WebGL. The building, its specifications and its location are fictional.

Original models

Every façade panel, tree and car is generated from code and stored as compressed GLB files, with no third-party models or textures.

Real-time light

Physically based materials, a physical sky for environment lighting, soft shadows, ambient occlusion and bloom.

Built for every screen

Touch gestures, keyboard controls and adaptive resolution, with a still image where WebGL is not available.
